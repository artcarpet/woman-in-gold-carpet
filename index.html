OFFICIAL UNVEILING

Presented by the Austrian Ambassador in Tehran

On 23 January 2018, Woman in Gold – A Persian Handwoven Masterpiece was officially unveiled at Parsian Hotel, Tehran.

The ceremony was presented by Dr. Stefan Scholz, Austrian Ambassador to Iran, celebrating the artwork as a cultural bridge between Gustav Klimt's artistic legacy and the centuries-old tradition of Persian carpet weaving.

This official event marked the beginning of the artwork's international journey.

Our Story

Honoring the original masterpiece through a new artistic dimension.

This handwoven masterpiece is more than a carpet. It is the meeting point of Persian craftsmanship, fine art, and cultural heritage.

Inspired by one of the world's most celebrated paintings, every knot reflects months of dedication, precision, and artistic vision.

Created as a unique work, this work represents a dialogue between cultures. This tapestry recreates a famous work of art in a different way by artists from the Orient.

Today, this work of art is designed as an artistic asset for a new generation of collectors and art enthusiasts.
