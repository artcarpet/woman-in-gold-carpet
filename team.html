The tapestry rug "Woman in Gold" is the result of design and weaving by our team of artists and weavers, presented with respect to admirers of Gustav Klimt's artwork. This piece has been released for direct sale to the buyer as an NFT, and ownership of the physical artwork transfers to the buyer upon purchase of the NFT.
