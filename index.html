Behind the Masterpiece

Where Persian craftsmanship meets timeless art

Inspired by one of the most celebrated artistic visions of the twentieth century, this handwoven masterpiece reimagines classical beauty through the language of Persian carpet weaving.

Crafted over a period of fifteen months by master weavers, this artwork embodies patience, precision, and centuries of craftsmanship.
