Official Website of the 1/1 NFT Artwork

This artistic masterpiece was officially unveiled by the Ambassador of Austria to Iran on January 23, 2018, at the Parsian Hotel, Tehran.

Artist Statement

Woman in Gold Carpet is a unique 1/1 NFT artwork inspired by the timeless beauty of Persian carpet weaving. It combines cultural heritage with contemporary digital art and is permanently preserved on the Polygon blockchain.
